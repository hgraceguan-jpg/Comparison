⚠️ Key Flags & Considerations
⚠ VietJet (Option D) — Cheapest But With Conditions
At ₱34,120 it appears cheapest, but requires: (1) 3:35 AM departure from Manila — means waking up at 1–2 AM; (2) fixed Wednesday–Saturday travel only, no flexibility; (3) mandatory visits to Latex shop, Silk shop, Treasure shop, and Jewelry shop as a condition of the group rate — the group MUST visit these. These are commission-based stops that take time from actual sightseeing.
⚠ Agency 3D2N Options (A & B) — Shorter Trip, More Limited
Options A (₱47,008) and B (₱41,008) are 3 days/2 nights with late 9:30 PM arrival on Day 1 — effectively only 2 full days. Hoi An is NOT included in either. You see Ba Na Hills + Da Nang city only. Option B (June) is the better value of the two if the group prefers a shorter trip.
ℹ DIY Advantage: Full Day 1 Since Early Arrival
DIY (Aug 6 flight) arrives at 7:40 AM, giving a full first day. Agency Cebu Pacific options arrive at 9:30 PM — Day 1 is essentially lost to travel. The DIY 4D3N is more productive than the agency 3D2N in terms of actual sightseeing time, even though the days are the same count.
✅ DIY Budget Strategy: ₱39,499 vs Agency Options
The DIY Budget Strategy (Bus + Shuttle + Walk) comes in at ₱39,499 — cheaper than 5 out of 5 agency options when including all actual costs. Unlike VietJet's ₱34,120 (which has mandatory shop stops + early departure), the DIY plan has no strings attached and gives 100% flexibility to enjoy Da Nang at your own pace.
